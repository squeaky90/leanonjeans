Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This policy explains what information we collect when you request a quote on this page, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We've written it in plain language.

What we collect

When you fill out the quote form, we collect the information you choose to give us:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your email address (if you provide it)
  • Your ZIP code, and later your street address if you share it on a call
  • The type of insurance you'd like quoted (renters, auto, home, life or business)
  • Anything else you choose to tell us, like the best time to call, how you heard about us, or notes

Like most websites, this page uses Google Analytics, which may set cookies and record basic information about your visit, such as the pages you view, whether you submitted the form, or whether you tapped a call or text button. This helps us understand how people find us and which of our ads work. You can limit this tracking in your browser or Google account settings.

Why we collect it

We use your information to prepare an insurance quote for you and to contact you to discuss it. We don't use it for anything unrelated to your request.

How we contact you

We'll call or text you only if you've given us permission by checking the consent box on the form, or by contacting us first. Giving consent is never required to buy insurance. If you'd like us to stop contacting you, just tell us on a call or reply STOP to any text.

We don't sell your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as needed to prepare your quote, for example with the insurance company we're quoting through, and with the service providers that help us run this page and receive form submissions.

How to have your information deleted

You can ask us at any time to delete the information you sent through this page. Call our office at (901) 346-0300 and we'll take care of it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
